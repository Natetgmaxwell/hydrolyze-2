## The slice (spec §Slice 2)

- **Each swimmer's drop-rule standard = their zone pace from `TrainingZoneService` at the zone term**, if they have a usable profile. The zone pace replaces the rep-1 standard (the "predicted standard" — the drop rule compares the rep against this standard, strikes if rep > standard + threshold).

- **Zone resolution failure never crashes**: no usable profile / fetch failure / unparseable pace → rep-1 fallback.

Acceptance (spec): "Simulator: zone set with a mixed squad — profiled swimmers show their zone pace as target; unprofiled swimmers time normally with predicted targets; drop rule evaluates profiled swimmers against the zone pace."

## The design decision (zone standard vs maturity gate)

The zone pace is authoritative (from a usable 3MT profile trial ≥ 2). It does NOT go through the `Prediction.Maturity` gate — that gate exists because statistical predictions from sparse recorded times are uncertain; a zone pace is a direct physiological prescription. So:
- `zoneStandardMs != nil` → `standard = zoneStandardMs`, `source = .zone`, no maturity gate.
- `zoneStandardMs == nil` → existing behavior (predicted if maturity ≥ reliable, else rep1).

This is the cleanest way to satisfy "drop rule evaluates profiled swimmers against the zone pace" — the standard IS the zone pace for a zone set with a profile, and the rep-1 fallback is the "never a phantom strike" guarantee for unprofiled swimmers (they get rep-1, exactly as today).
