How it works
  • For each swimmer, the engine knows their expected time for this set (based on their training data, or their PBs, or a population average as fallback)
  • For each tap, it finds the swimmer with the smallest gap between tap time and expected time
  • ⚠ Flag: if the best gap is more than 4 seconds, the engine flags it — something might be off
  • ? Flag: if the top two swimmers are within 2× of each other, the engine can't decide — coach resolves manually
  • Swimmer names come from Supabase (real data). You can override expected times below.
