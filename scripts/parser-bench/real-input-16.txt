Final major race rehearsal of the week. It gives each event group a specific main set while everyone completes the same preparation, skills and warm-down.

The emphasis is exact race execution—not exhausting. Every quality repetition should be timed.

Thursday PM — Broken-Race Quality

Intensity: High quality with generous recovery
Purpose: Race pace, broken-race execution, turns, underwater skills and back-end speed

Deck activation — 10 minutes

Complete two controlled rounds:

* 10 band pull-aparts
* 8 external rotations each arm
* 10 straight-arm pulldowns
* 8 band stroke pulls each arm
* 8 ankle pogo jumps
* 4 streamline squat jumps
* 3 explosive start jumps

Rest for approximately 20 seconds between exercises. Use light bands and keep the jumps sharp rather than fatiguing.

1. General warm-up

* 200m relaxed choice
* 4 × 50m as 25m drill + 25m swim @ 1:00
* 4 × 50m as 25m kick + 25m swim @ 1:00

Build the kick and swimming gradually from approximately 55–70%.

# 2. Stroke connection

## 6 × 50m @ 1:00–1:10

1. 25m front scull + 25m swim
2. 25m mid scull + 25m swim
3. 25m drill + 25m swim
4. 25m drill + 25m build
5. 15m strong connection + easy to wall
6. Full-stroke swimming at approximately 75%

use race stroke where appropriate.

3. Turn and breakout preparation

## 4 × 50m @ 1:10 — 200m

* 15m controlled into the wall
* Race-quality turn
* Fast underwater and breakout to 10–12.5m
* Easy to the next wall
* Repeat the skill

4 × 25m @ :45 — 100m

* Fast underwater and breakout
* Build to race tempo
* Easy into the wall

4. Race priming

Complete two rounds:

* 1 × 25m build to race tempo @ :40
* 1 × 25m race tempo from a push @ :45
* 1 × 50m as 25m smooth + 25m fast @ 1:10
* 100m easy @ 2:00

The fast sections should reach event-specific speed without producing substantial fatigue.

Group A — 50/100m athletes

Part 1: Race-speed 25s

Two rounds:

* 1 × 25m from a dive at 50m speed @ 1:00
* 1 × 25m from a push at 100m race pace @ :50
* 1 × 25m as 12.5m easy + 12.5m maximum finish @ :50
* 1 × 25m from a push, fast turn approach into the wall @ :50
* 100m easy @ 2:00

Take an additional 60 seconds between rounds.
Focus
* Dive 25: acceleration and breakout speed
* Push 25: sustainable 100m stroke rate
* Finish 25: back-end speed
* Turn approach: hold speed into the wall without gliding

Part 2: Broken race

50m specialists

Complete three rounds:

* 1 × broken 50m:

* 25m from a dive
* 15 seconds rest
* 25m from a push
* 150m very easy
* Additional 3–4 minutes before the next broken 50m

100m specialists

Complete two rounds:

* 1 × broken 100m:

* 50m from a dive
* 20 seconds rest
* 25m from a push
* 10 seconds rest
* 25m from a push
* 200m very easy
* Additional 4 minutes before round two

Record every segment and the total swimming time.

Part 3: Back-end quality

Two rounds:

* 1 × 50m as 25m at 100m pace + 25m faster @ 1:30
* 2 × 25m at final-25-of-100 effort @ :50
* 100m easy @ 2:00

Group B — 200m athletes

Part 1: Broken 200m rehearsals

Complete two rounds.
Broken 200m

* 75m at goal 200m pace
* 15 seconds rest
* 50m at goal 200m pace
* 10 seconds rest
* 50m at goal 200m pace
* 10 seconds rest
* 25m at back-end race effort

Then:
* 200m very easy
* Additional 4–5 minutes before round two
Round emphasis

1. Exact target pace and controlled opening speed
2. Maintain pace, then attack the final 50m

Record:
* Opening 75m
* Each 50m
* Final 25m
* Total swimming time
* Stroke count where possible

Part 2: Pace confirmation — 600m

Two rounds:

* 4 × 50m at goal 200m pace @ 1:15–1:30
* 100m easy @ 2:00
Round one

Hold the same target split across all four 50s.
Round two
* No. 1: First-50 race rhythm
* No. 2: Middle-50 rhythm
* No. 3: Third-50 discipline
* No. 4: Final-50 speed

The final repetition can be slightly faster, but it should not become an unstructured sprint.
Part 3: Finishing speed
* 4 × 25m at final-50-of-200 effort @ :50
* 100m easy

Group C — Short-course 400m athletes

Part 1: Goal-pace 50s

Two rounds:

* 4 × 50m at goal 400m pace @ 1:00–1:15
* 200m relaxed @ approximately 3:30
* Additional 60 seconds between rounds

Choose a cycle that provides approximately 15–25 seconds rest after each quality 50m.
Round emphasis

1. Exact pace, stroke count and turn quality
2. Hold pace with the final 15m of repetition four slightly stronger

For a goal time of 4:32:
* Goal average = 34.0 seconds per 50m
* Target range = approximately 33.7–34.5 from a push

Part 2: Controlled broken 400m — 800m
Broken 400m
* 100m at goal 400m pace
* 15 seconds rest
* 100m at goal 400m pace
* 15 seconds rest
* 100m at goal 400m pace
* 10 seconds rest
* 100m at goal pace, lifting the final 25m

Then:
400m very easy

The aim is not to produce an unrealistic broken-race time. The aim is to reproduce the actual race rhythm while maintaining turns, underwater distance and stroke length.

Part 3: Pace-change swimming
4 × 50m @ 1:10
* 25m at goal 400m pace
* 25m faster than goal 400m pace

Group D — Open-water athletes

Main set
4 × 400m threshold
Complete each 400m as:
* 100m controlled aerobic
* 100m moderate threshold
* 100m at threshold
* 100m strong

Use approximately:
* Freestyle cycle: 5:30–6:30
* Adjust the cycle to provide 20–40 seconds rest.

After each 400m:
* 1 × 100m easy @ 1:45–2:00

The easy 100m is included in the 2,000m total.
Round emphasis

1. Establish control and stroke length
2. Include sighting every six to eight strokes
3. Simulate a mid-race surge during the third 100m
4. Strong final 100m as though racing toward the finish

The final 100m should be strong but technically sustainable—not an all-out pool sprint.

# Common race-skills finish
4 × 50m @ 1:10 — 200m
* 25m easy into a race-quality turn
* Fast underwater and breakout
* Easy to the wall
4 × 25m @ :50 — 100m
* Odd: Fast breakout to 12.5m
* Even: 12.5m smooth + 12.5m fast finish

If the skills are deteriorating, keep these controlled rather than forcing additional speed.
Warm-down — 500m

* 200m relaxed choice
* 4 × 50m as 25m backstroke + 25m freestyle @ 1:00
* 4 × 25m long and relaxed @ :40

Encourage relaxed breathing, soft recovery and progressively lower stroke rate.

Important quality controls

* Allow approximately ±0.5 seconds per 50m and ±1 second per 100m.
* Stop a broken race if loses stroke integrity or is clearly unable to reproduce target pace.
* The first section must not be excessively fast.
* Record all broken-race segments, not only the total time.
* Give School Nationals swimmers extra recovery rather than extra repetitions.
* With competition approaching, successful execution is more valuable than accumulating fatigue.
