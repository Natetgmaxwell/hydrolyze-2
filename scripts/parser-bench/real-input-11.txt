Warm-up 

400 m easy freestyle

200 m choice stroke (easy)

4 × 50 m drill/swim by 25 m

4 × 25 m build to ~70% effort



Main Set 

Option 1: Continuous Aerobic

3 × 600 m freestyle

Zone 1–low Zone 2

45–60 s rest between repetitions

Focus on:

Long, relaxed stroke

Bilateral breathing (if appropriate)

Low stroke rate

Consistent pace

Option 2: Broken Aerobic

12 × 200 m freestyle

15–20 s rest

Hold a comfortable, even pace throughout

Target:

RPE: 2–3/10

Heart rate: ~60–70% HRmax

No lactate accumulation





Technique Set

Choose drills based on the squad's needs:

8 × 50 m as 25 m drill + 25 m swim

Examples:

Catch-up

Fingertip drag

Single-arm freestyle

Sculling

      

Optional Speed Activation 

8 × 25 m

Smooth build to 75–80% effort

Full recovery (30–45 s)

Emphasise excellent technique, not maximal speed

      

Cool-down 

Easy choice stroke

Relaxed swimming with long strokes

Gentle kicking if desired





Focus

Keep effort aerobic throughout.

Maintain stroke length and rhythm rather than chasing speed.

Avoid accumulating fatigue; should leave the pool feeling refreshed.

Monitor stroke count and pace consistency to ensure efficiency is maintained as the session progresses.
