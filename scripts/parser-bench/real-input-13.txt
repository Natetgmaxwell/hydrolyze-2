Race-pace session

Warm-up — 1,200m

400m swim, building each 100m

4 × 50m kick @ 1:00, descend 1–4

4 × 50m drill/swim by 25m @ 1:00

4 × 50m as 25m build + 25m easy @ 1:00

4 × 25m underwater breakout into easy speed @ :40

4 × 25m at 200m race rhythm @ :40

100m easy



Activation set



2 rounds:

2 × 50m at 200m goal pace @ 1:10

2 × 25m at 100m goal pace @ :45

50m easy @ 1:15



The pace must be controlled and technically clean. This prepares you without creating significant lactate.



Main set 

Part 1: 200m race pace 



3 rounds:

4 × 50m at goal 200m pace @ 1:10–1:20

100m easy recovery @ 2:00

#Take an additional 1 minute between rounds if necessary.

-Target: Each 50m should equal one quarter of the swimmer's goal 200m time, allowing for the difference between a dive start and a push.



Example for a goal time of 2:08:

Goal average = 32.0 seconds per 50m

Push target could be approximately 32.0–32.5

The final 50m must remain within approximately 0.5 seconds of the first



Part 2: 100m race pace 

3 rounds:

4 × 25m at goal 100m pace @ :45–:50 The 25s should reflect the middle section of the 100m—not an all-out dive 25.

100m very easy @ 2:00



Example for a goal 100m time of 58.0:

Target average = approximately 14.5 seconds per 25m

Push repetitions may need to be adjusted slightly for the missing dive advantage

      

Part 3: Back-end speed 

2 rounds:

1 × 50m as 25m at 200 pace + 25m at 100 pace @ 1:30 Focus on maintaining stroke length and body position while increasing rate.

2 × 25m at final-25-of-100 effort @ :50

50m easy @ 1:30







Skills finish — 400m

4 × 50m as 15m fast breakout + easy to the wall @ 1:10

4 × 25m turn entry, fast turn and breakout to 10–12.5m @ :45

100m easy

Swim-down — 600m

200m relaxed choice

4 × 50m backstroke/free by 25m @ 1:00

4 × 25m long and smooth @ :35

100m easy

Option B: 200/400m swimmers



These swimmers complete the same warm-up, but their main set develops 400m pace control before progressing to 200m speed.



Activation set 

2 rounds:

2 × 50m at 400m goal pace @ 1:00

2 × 25m at 200m goal pace @ :40

50m easy @ 1:10



Main set 

Part 1: 400m race pace 

3 rounds:

4 × 100m at goal 400m pace @ 1:40–2:00

100m easy @ 2:00



Allow an additional 1 minute between rounds if needed to preserve exact pace.



Example for a goal 400m time of 4:32:

Goal average = 1:08 per 100m

Target range = approximately 1:07.5–1:08.5

You should not race the first repetition and then deteriorate

Suggested round emphasis:

Controlled pace and stroke count

Exact pace with race-quality turns

Hold pace while lifting the final 25m of each 100



Part 2: 200m race pace

2 rounds:

4 × 50m at goal 200m pace @ 1:10–1:20

100m easy @ 2:00

You should lift stroke rate from 400m rhythm to 200m rhythm without shortening the stroke excessively.

2 rounds:

1 × 100m as:50m at 400 pace/25m at 200 pace/25m faster than 200 pace

2 × 50m easy @ 1:00



Give the quality 100m approximately 3 minutes before beginning the next round.
