SC State - conditioning with drill progressions, kicking, and race-quality turns. The goal is to keep heart rate in the aerobic zone while maintaining excellent technique throughout.

4,000 m Aerobic Technique Set

 Warm-up 800

* 400 Swim 100 Free / 100 Back × 2 - Build effort each 100
* 200 Pull Paddle + buoy* Long strokes, high elbow catch
* 200 Kick 50 streamline flutter* 50 side kick
  * Repeat

---

Drill Progression

**12 × 50 @ 10–15 s rest**1- 4: 25 drill + 25 swim Focus: catch
* 5–8: 25 drill + 25 swim-Focus: rotation and bodyline
* 9–12: 25 drill + 25 swim Focus: distance per stroke
Every 50**Race-quality turn, Streamline to 10–15, Clean breakout

Main Set

**3 rounds of:**

3 x 200 Freestyle aerobic 75-80% 2:20-2:30
  * Hold a consistent aerobic pace
  * Descend 1–3 slightly
  * Focus on: * Stroke count, Even pacing, Fast turns, Strong underwater off every wall
2 × 50 Kick #1 moderate #2 build to strong finish - Streamline every wall

Aerobic Finish

6 x 100 @ 15 s 
* Odds: Negative split, Long strokes
* Evens: First 50 aerobic, Flip/open turn, Underwater to 15 m, Last 50 strong aerobic

swim down 200

* Easy choice swim
* Mix strokes
* Relaxed breathing and long strokes

 Emphasis

* Every turn should be executed at race quality, even during aerobic swimming.
* Prioritise stroke length before stroke rate.
* Maintain consistent pacing across the 200s, with only a slight increase in speed on each repeat.
* Use the kick sets to reinforce body position and streamline, not just leg endurance.
* Encourage to hit the same stroke count (±1 stroke) on each 50 within the aerobic repeats.
