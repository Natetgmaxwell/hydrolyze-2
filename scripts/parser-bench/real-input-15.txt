### Underwater 25s

* Push in a tight streamline
* Controlled underwater distance to 8–12.5m
* Smooth breakout
* Easy swimming to the wall

Do not turn the underwater work into maximal breath-holding.

4. Competition-skills block

A. Turn preparation — 4 × 50m @ 1:10 — 200m

* 15m smooth swimming into the wall
* Race-quality turn
* Underwater and breakout to 10–12.5m
* Easy to the opposite wall

Focus on:
* Maintaining speed into the wall
* No unnecessary glide
* Fast foot placement
* Tight streamline
* Breakout timed into the first stroke

B. Start and breakout — 4 × 25m @ 1:00 — 100m by BBB

* Dive or race-specific start
* Fast to 12.5–15m
* Easy to the wall

Progress the four starts:
1. Approximately 70%
2. Approximately 80%
3. Approximately 85%
4. Approximately 90%

There should be no straining or forced maximal effort.

C. Race-rhythm swimming — 4 × 50m @ 1:15–1:30 — 200m

1. 25m at 200m race rhythm + 25m easy
2. 25m easy + 25m at 200m race rhythm
3. 15m at 100m tempo + easy to the wall
4. 50m smooth, perfect competition stroke

"Race rhythm" means reproducing the event's stroke rate, body position and timing. It does not mean racing the repetition.

5. Recovery pull and reset — 400m

4 × 100m pull @ 1:45–2:00

* No. 1: Long and relaxed
* No. 2: Negative split
* No. 3: Choice of breathing pattern
* No. 4: Descend the stroke count across each 25m

Use a pull buoy only. Avoid paddles unless there is no shoulder tightness

Intensity should remain around 60–65%.

6. Warm-down — 400m

* 200m easy backstroke
* 200m easy freestyle
* 4 × 25m choice drill @ :40
* 200m very easy choice

On the final 100m, to lower their heart rate, relax breathing and finish with the best possible stroke shape.
