**Status:** LOCKED. Owner: build profile. Branch: v2.0.

**Grounding verified 2026-08-18:** `set_rest_events` table ships with V0.39. `AnalyticsService` exists (training bands, gear breakdown, swimmer improvement). No recovery/rest analytics anywhere.

## Why

The drop rule (V0.39) creates a byproduct: per-swimmer rest-extension events — "how many reps at what rest before times fall." That's a recovery signature no hardware product can accumulate, because no hardware sits in the coach's hand at the wall. The surface ships early and fills with data (research doc: "ship the surface early, let it fill"). This is the flywheel that makes Hydrolyze's history a moat.

## The insight, in coach terms

Every drop-rule strike is a datapoint: swimmer X, set Y, rep N, +0.6s off standard, needed +30s rest. Across weeks, the pattern answers: "Does Ariana hold pace longer than Jack at the same rest? Is she dropping earlier as the season wears on?" — the recovery signature. The coach sees it without tallying anything; the engine counted.

## Contract

### Slice 1 — Analytics: recovery signature query

Extend `AnalyticsService` (or a sibling `RecoveryService` — match the existing service pattern):

- Per swimmer, per week: total strikes, strikes per 100 reps, avg delta off standard, avg extended rest, revert rate.
- Two views:
  1. **Trend:** strikes-per-week over the last 8 weeks (line, coach vocabulary: "recovery strain").
  2. **Signature:** per swimmer, "typical rep count before dropping" — the median rep_index at first strike across anaerobic sets (the recovery signature number).

Acceptance:
- Unit tests on the aggregation (pure SQL or service-layer mapping): empty data → empty state; known fixture → correct medians and rates.
- Live query returns rows once V0.39 has produced events.

### Slice 2 — Display: swimmer detail + squad glance

Per-swimmer on the swimmer detail view (existing surface — no new squad view; that's the Data Center follow-up):

- One line: "Drops on rep 3–4 of hard sets — recovers in one rep." (the signature, in coach words)
- A small trend sparkline if 8 weeks of data exist; hidden otherwise (never greyed-out empty).

Anti-patterns (explicitly avoided):
- No statistical jargon ("median rep_index", "revert rate") — coach vocabulary
- No confidence percentages
- No modal surfaces
- No surface when data is absent — hidden, not "no data yet" chrome

Acceptance:
- Simulator: swimmer with seeded rest events shows the signature line; swimmer without shows nothing new.

### Slice 3 — The "reverted" signal (coach-is-authority feedback loop)

Aggregate revert rate and surface it subtly on the coach's review: if the coach reverts a specific swimmer's extensions >30% of the time, the engine flags "Ariana's extensions are usually wrong — check the standard" (one inline line, never modal). The engine learns the coach disagrees; it never silently stops extending.

Acceptance:
- Fixture with 4/10 reverted events for one swimmer → the flag appears for that swimmer only.
