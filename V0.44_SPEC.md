## What stays (locked design rules — do NOT touch)

- **JetBrains Mono for all numeric/data** (timing display, PBs, lap times, rep counts, distances) — the brand signature
- **B&W + 20% cyan** — the timing screen aesthetic; the cyan flash on LAP is the only loud color moment
- **True black background** — pool-deck glare contrast (deliberate, per the June design direction)
- **Single-button timing ergonomics** (LAP circle + STOP label) — the audit's "character" list
- **The Haptics/PressFeedback/Animations vocabulary** — extend it, never bypass it (one vocabulary per file rule)

## Character injections (grounded in gbrain)

1. **Coach voice copy** — the app speaks like someone who coaches at 5am with 24 swimmers: short, warm, specific. "Plan a session" not "Schedule Workout". "Who's in today" not "View Swimmers". Empty states say something real.
2. **Warmth through motion** — the June direction: spring physics everywhere (Animations.springSnappy/springSoft, never plain easeInOut), idle CTAs breathe, rows animate in/out, state transitions crossfade. Fill the gaps the June audit deferred (state transitions in TimingView, row enter/exit in lists).
3. **The wordmark** — JetBrains Mono Bold "Hydrolyze" in the sidebar. The brand font as the brand.
4. **Status as color** — draft/scheduled/complete dots on workout rows (tokens, not raw hex).
