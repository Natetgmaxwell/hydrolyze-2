## Objective

Re-skin Hydrolyze into the deep-teal system so it reads as a designed instrument, not an AI default: pool-water monochrome, one fire-coloured action per screen, every number in JetBrains Mono, and microinteractions that carry the warmth (per the locked "warm through motion" direction). The app should feel like a joy to use at 5am on a pool deck.

## Typography law

- **If it's a measurement, it's mono**: time, distance, pace, stroke rate, counts, percentages, deltas. `numeric*` roles, tabular.
- Names, sentences, section titles: SF Pro. The mono/SF split is the "instrument panel vs human voice" rule — never swap.
- Wordmark stays JetBrains Mono Bold (V0.44).

## Microinteraction & character (extends the existing vocabulary)

1. **Timing screen** becomes bgDeep; stopwatch digits mist; LAP flash goes cyan→coral (the only loud colour moment stays exactly one).
2. **`numericText()` roll** on every value change (PBs, counts, deltas) — digits roll in place, never jump width (mono law).
3. **PB moments** get the tick-mark celebration + success haptic (exists) — recoloured coral-on-mist.
4. **Breathing** stays on idle CTAs only (exists). Row enter/exit springs stay (V0.44 shipped).
5. **New: haptic on set-progress milestone** during timing (set halfway / final rep) — `Haptics.success()` + a subtle progress bar fill animation. Scales with Weber-Fechner: the final rep's beat is stronger than halfway.
6. **Charts**: axis labels + series values → mono; series strokes from the ramp (teal/fog/mist); delta annotations coral/amber by sign.

### B. Applied principles (Hormozi × Sutherland — for flow-level work, out of V0.46 scope)

**Hormozi** (*$100M Offers*): value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time × Effort) — the parse moment is where the equation is won; perception is reality (surface progress, don't assume it's inferred); engineer an early emotional win (first 60s of every workflow); name + itemise the value stack (parent report as named artefact, not "a PDF"); proof over claims (lead with trend evidence).

**Sutherland** (*Alchemy*): psycho-logic beats logic (felt-right > arguable-right — the brief's whole thesis); psychological moonshots — perceived quality is cheapest to buy in waiting/transition moments; the opposite of a good idea can be good (keep the original pasted text toggleable after parse — the visible "before" makes the parser look brilliant); costly signalling — visible craft on the parent-facing surface is the premium signal; solve the perception problem before the engineering problem (3MT entry as ritual, not fewer fields).

**Their tension:** Hormozi A/B-tests the flourish; Sutherland warns what converts in 5 minutes isn't what builds attachment over a season. Our tiebreak: the coach is the user, the season is the timeframe — favour Sutherland on character, Hormozi on the parse moment.

### C. Folded into V0.46 slices (zero flow changes)

- Slice 2: mono justification is alignment/jitter (honest framing); hundredths weight-step pilot.
- Slice 3: rep dots (discrete steps) not progress bars; any bar accelerates final 20%; no motion on frequent controls.
- Slice 4/5: CTA bottom-anchored + ≥44pt audit incl. dense set rows; no-loading-UI preference; "does it carry state?" animation test.
